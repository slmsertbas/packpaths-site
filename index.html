PackPaths

A cozy van-packing puzzle for iPhone. Pack Mira's delivery van so every shop on the route gets its parcels, one stop after another, and watch the van drive the street.

  • Five chapters of sixteen vans, plus four daily puzzles and a weekly route.
  • Hints that help without giving the answer away.
  • Plays offline. No account needed. English and Turkish.
