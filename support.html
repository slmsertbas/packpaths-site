Restore a purchase

Open the van's ⋯ menu, then Settings, then Unlock, and tap Restore Purchases. Use the Apple Account that bought the unlock. The unlock removes ads, makes every hint free and opens the Cold Quay chapter.

A delivery is blocked

Only the current stop's parcels can leave. They must slide straight toward the rear door. Use Restart, Undo or the lightbulb hint to try another layout.

Ads, and changing your ad choices

Ads appear between some vans and are optional. A rewarded video plays only when you ask for an exact-spot hint. In Settings you can open the ad privacy choices where they apply. On your iPhone you can also change tracking permission under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

My progress looks wrong

Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes the progress. Before you reset anything, email me with what you see.

Report a problem

Please include the app version, your iPhone model, your iOS version, the van or puzzle, and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or full purchase receipts.
